## 🎬 THE EXPERIENCE

**What if an eye could become an entire universe?**

EYE → SPACE is a Python-powered cinematic ASCII animation. It begins with a human eye, moves through a dramatic iris and pupil zoom, and transforms into a glowing cosmic scene featuring stars, a nebula, and a heart.

## 🌠 VISUAL JOURNEY

| Stage | Animation |
|:---:|:---|
| 👁️ 01 | Human eye opens |
| 🌑 02 | Eye blinks and moves |
| 🔍 03 | Iris and pupil zoom |
| ✨ 04 | Stars fill the screen |
| 🌌 05 | Nebula transforms |
| ❤️ 06 | A glowing cosmic heart appears |

---

## ⚡ FEATURES

- 👁️ Cinematic eye-opening animation
- 🔍 Smooth iris and pupil zoom effects
- 🌈 Colourful ASCII rendering
- 🌌 Procedural nebula and cosmic effects
- ⭐ 3,200 animated star particles
- ☄️ Shooting stars and comet effects
- ❤️ Animated cosmic heart transformation
- ✍️ Typewriter-style developer credits
- 🎞️ Target animation speed: 30 FPS
